      Glossary & Methodology

      SereneScore

      SereneScore is a forward-looking predictive index (0–1000) estimating financial resilience.

      SereneIDs

      SereneIDs are our behavioural context signals that help explain how and why an individual’s trajectory is changing. They come in two forms.
      SereneID Life Tags are dynamic indicators reflecting an individuals broader personal circumstances (e.g., employment status, family structure).
      SereneID Vulnerability Markers specifically flag behavioural signals likely to lead to future financial distress and are aligned with FCA definitions of vulnerability.

      Risk Bands

      Risk bands are calculated relatively, with the top 5% of the national population designated as critical, and the following 10% and 20% as high and medium respectively. This enables cohort risk profile comparison with a clear national benchmark.

      Data Population

      The dataset contains individuals who have consented to open-banking data aggregation. Because consent is temporal, total population composition shifts dynamically month-to-month.
